DARK FUNK DURCAL SYNT v2 (macOS) - Created at Polaris Sound Lab, Merida, Yucatan, Mexico

ENGLISH
1. Right-click "Install.command" > Open > Open (macOS asks once because it was downloaded).
2. Restart Ableton Live. Settings > Plug-Ins: turn on "Use Audio Units v2" and/or "Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders", press Rescan.
3. KeyLab Essential: Settings > Link, Tempo & MIDI > Control Surface = DFD_KeyLab_Essential, Input and Output = KeyLab Essential.
   In the MIDI ports list, turn on Track and Remote for the KeyLab Essential input.
4. Drag "Dark Funk Durcal Synt" (Plug-Ins > Audio Units or VST3 > Polaris Sound Lab) onto a MIDI track. Press PLAY.
The full illustrated manual (English / Spanish) comes with the download.

ESPANOL
1. Clic derecho en "Install.command" > Abrir > Abrir (macOS lo pregunta una vez porque se descargo de internet).
2. Reinicia Ableton Live. Settings > Plug-Ins: activa "Use Audio Units v2" y/o "Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders" y pulsa Rescan.
3. KeyLab Essential: Settings > Link, Tempo & MIDI > Control Surface = DFD_KeyLab_Essential, Input y Output = KeyLab Essential.
   En la lista de puertos MIDI activa Track y Remote para la entrada KeyLab Essential.
4. Arrastra "Dark Funk Durcal Synt" (Plug-Ins > Audio Units o VST3 > Polaris Sound Lab) a una pista MIDI. Pulsa PLAY.
El manual ilustrado completo (ingles / espanol) viene con la descarga.
