DARK FUNK DURCAL SYNT v2 - Created at Polaris Sound Lab, Merida, Yucatan, Mexico
Free and open source (AGPL-3.0) - https://github.com/drophlex/dfd-synth-plugin
AU / VST3 for any DAW (Ableton Live, Logic, FL Studio, Bitwig, Reaper, Cubase, Studio One...) + a standalone app.

ESPANOL - GRATIS Y DE CODIGO ABIERTO
Mac:     clic derecho en "Install.command" > Abrir > Abrir (macOS lo pregunta una vez).
Windows: doble clic en "Install.bat" y acepta el permiso de administrador.
Reinicia tu DAW y busca "Dark Funk Durcal Synt" (Polaris Sound Lab). Arrastralo a una pista MIDI y pulsa SUMMON.
KeyLab Essential + Ableton: conectalo y Live lo enlaza solo. Si no: Settings > Link, Tempo & MIDI >
Control Surface = DFD_KeyLab_Essential, Input/Output = KeyLab Essential, Track y Remote en ON.
Si te gusta lo que hago, puedes apoyar con una donacion por PayPal: drophlex@gmail.com

ENGLISH - FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
Mac:     right-click "Install.command" > Open > Open (macOS asks once).
Windows: double-click "Install.bat" and accept the administrator prompt.
Restart your DAW and find "Dark Funk Durcal Synt" (Polaris Sound Lab). Drag it onto a MIDI track and press SUMMON.
KeyLab Essential + Ableton: plug it in, Live links it by itself.
If you like it, you can support it with a PayPal donation: drophlex@gmail.com

Guide and manual (EN/ES): https://drophlex.github.io/dark-funk-durcal-synt-fm1/plugin.html
